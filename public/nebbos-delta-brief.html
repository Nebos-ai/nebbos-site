    Scale of the AI economy in 2026

    The AI economy is compounding across every measurable dimension: capex, compute, users, revenue, and valuations. But the growth rates are not evenly distributed — the layers commoditizing fastest are exactly the ones capturing the smallest share of durable value.

    Three-year price collapse at the model layer

    The single most important operational fact of the AI market: frontier model prices have fallen more in three years than cloud storage prices did in twenty. This is unprecedented commoditization.

    The paradox — Anthropic revenue grew 14.6× during the same 36 months

    Prices fell 99.7% at the flagship tier. Volume grew fast enough at the winning labs to overwhelm the compression: Anthropic's Q2 2026 revenue of $11.5B is 14.6× the Q2 2025 figure of $787M. Since average token pricing dropped ~70% over the same period, the implied volume growth is roughly 40–50×. This is the specific dynamic that predicts where AI value pools next — infrastructure (to serve the volume) and durable data owners (whose data feeds the volume), not commoditizing middle-tier tokens.
