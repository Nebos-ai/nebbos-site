Where a Nebbos deployment shows up

Three departments carry the day-to-day lift in a regulated financial firm. Each gets its own scoped Pearl trained on your own ratified decisions. Each writes to the same tamper-evident chain, and each is isolated from the others at the database, not the application.

Department 01
Compliance operations
Pearl trained on ratified policy decisions plus your last two years of investigation outcomes. Every recommendation names the specific policy applied, the past cases it resembles, and the source documents cited.
Department 02
Risk & monitoring
Cross-domain signals from the Orchestrator surface where a monitoring rule and an investigation trend contradict. Acknowledgement carries the human-token attribution — the substrate refuses acknowledgements that omit it.
Department 03
Regulatory affairs
Every escalation to the CCO is an approval graph with per-step tier gates. Every approval lands in the audit chain as a signed record — not a checkbox in a form.
Substrate
Row-level isolation at the database
Departments cannot read each other's records. Not an application check — a database predicate that rewrites every query. A bug in the UI cannot cross a scope boundary.
