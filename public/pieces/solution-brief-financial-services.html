Nebbos for regulated financial services.

Nebbos is an institutional operations intelligence platform. In regulated financial services deployments, the platform supports compliance operations, risk monitoring, and regulatory affairs with department-scoped reasoning, cryptographically-attested approvals, and a tamper-evident audit chain that satisfies chain-of-custody requirements without vendor mediation.

Where Nebbos is deployed

A regulated financial services deployment typically spans three departments. Each department operates a Pearl trained on the institution's ratified policies and prior investigation outcomes. Reasoning provenance accompanies every recommendation. Row-level isolation is enforced at the database layer.

Department 01
Compliance operations
The compliance Pearl operates on ratified policy decisions and trailing investigation outcomes. Each recommendation includes citations to applied policy, comparable prior cases, and referenced source documents. Reviewers disposition cases with the full reasoning trail present rather than reconstructed.
Department 02
Risk and monitoring
The Orchestrator surfaces cross-domain signals where monitoring rules and investigation patterns diverge. Signal acknowledgement requires the human-token attribution tuple; unattested acknowledgements are refused at the substrate.
Department 03
Regulatory affairs
Escalations to the Chief Compliance Officer traverse approval graphs with per-step tier gates. Each approval is recorded in the audit chain as a substrate-verified signature.
Substrate
Row-level isolation at the database
Departments cannot read one another's records. The isolation policy is enforced at the database via native row-security machinery; the application layer cannot bypass it. Queries against another department's scope are refused before an answer is composed.

Deployment path

Nebbos enterprise deployments execute in six phases over approximately 33 business days from kickoff to production sign-off. Each phase produces a signed exit artefact. Production sign-off requires written attestation from the operator's security officer against internal control requirements.

Compliance posture

Compliance status is maintained at nebbos.ai/docs/compliance/compliance-posture and updated as certifications advance. SOC 2 Type II is in progress. ISO 27001:2022 substrate controls are implemented; the information security management system is in preparation. The EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation pack is being assembled ahead of the 2027-08-02 obligation date. HIPAA is in readiness posture with the substrate safeguards implemented; BAA execution follows scoped assessment. GDPR/CCPA data-processing addendum is available.

Scoped assessment

The Nebbos deployment team produces a committee-ready assessment against the specific operational profile of a prospective deployment under NDA. Timeline: ten business days from initial scoping. Sections 01, 02, 03, 04, and 07 of the business case template are pre-populated by the team.
